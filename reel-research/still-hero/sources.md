# Still hero: background image sources

Research images for the light hero background mockups. Downloaded 28 Oct 2026.
Morris files came from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org). Unsplash files came from images.unsplash.com at `?w=2400&q=85`.
Files over 1.5 MB were re-encoded as JPEG at quality 85 and scaled down until they fit.
