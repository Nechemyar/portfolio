Choice Inventory

An inventory company in London. Landlords wanted a price before they’d pick up the phone.

Raffy runs inventory checks for letting agents, landlords and tenants across London. His prices depend on the size of the place and the extras, so every new client had to message him and wait for a number. I built a site that shows the price straight away, then hands people to Raffy on WhatsApp.
