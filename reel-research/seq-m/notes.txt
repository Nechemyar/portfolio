Mobile reel image-sequence takes (captured 2026-10-29, scripts/capture-m.mjs + takes-m.mjs in sumi-reel)
=======================================================================================================

Format: 1206x2622 (402x874 CSS at deviceScaleFactor 3), 30 fps, H.264 CRF 16, yuv420p.
Context: isMobile, hasTouch, iPhone Safari user agent (iOS 18.5).
No cursor or finger is drawn. <take>.cursor.json has per-frame {f, x, y, down, scrollY} in CSS px
(multiply by 3 for frame pixels), plus "marks" (the action times listed below) and "input": "touch".

How it was captured (same method as the desktop takes in ../seq/notes.txt):
- Playwright's fake clock, paused; every frame steps exactly 1/30 s. Every CSS animation,
  transition and element.animate() is paused and seeked to virtual time.
- Taps are REAL touch: CDP Input.dispatchTouchEvent touchStart, 3 held frames (down=true), touchEnd,
  so the browser synthesises the click itself. The slider drag is a real touch drag (touchMove stream).
  The press-and-hold is a real held touch.
- Between touches the log has an eased pointer path (slight arc, at most 70 CSS px per frame,
  0.15-0.45 s depending on distance) so an arrow or touch dot can be drawn travelling.
- Swipe-scrolls are programmatic eased scrolls (ease-out, like a light fling); the logged finger
  is down and moves 1:1 with the content for the first 45-50% of the swipe, then lifts. A real touch
  fling runs on the compositor's wall clock and would break the frame-exact timing.
- Each take starts with 0.3 s of still frames. The gap after a reaction settles before the next input
  is about 0.4 s. Every take was checked frame by frame on contact sheets (every 3rd frame).

1. jsense-modes.mp4 (8.27 s). jsense.org.uk, home page, accessibility widget
   0.00  still (hero: "Navigating the SEND journey, together")
   0.70  tap the round blue accessibility button (bottom right). The bottom sheet slides up
         over ~85% of the screen in about 0.2 s; the page behind dims.
   1.97  tap Reading font -> Atkinson Hyperlegible (the whole sheet's type changes; subtle)
   2.73  tap Reading font again -> OpenDyslexic (big, wide letterforms everywhere)
   3.70  tap High Contrast: the sheet goes white with heavy black outlines on every card
   4.90  tap Dark Contrast: everything flips to near-black with blue outlines (biggest flip)
   6.27  tap the X: the sheet closes and the whole page shows in dark contrast + OpenDyslexic
         (pink headline on black, yellow button). Holds to 8.27.
   SURPRISING: on phones the panel is a bottom sheet, so the mode changes show mostly ON the sheet;
   the payoff of seeing the page itself transformed comes when it closes at 6.27.
   High Contrast must come BEFORE Dark Contrast: after Dark, a High Contrast tap only outlines
   one card (barely visible), which is what the first attempt did.

         bottom of the screen and rolls £144 -> £168 -> £186 during the drag.
   2.93  tap Furnished (-> £216)

   4.63  tap Check-in (-> £258)

   In the first attempt the Check-in tap landed on that bar and changed nothing.

3. clinical-team.mp4 (4.20 s). clinicalassessments.co.uk/team/ (Meet The Team)
   0.00  still: "Meet The Team" heading and Dr. Yehuda Marshall's card
   0.50  swipe up 646 px (0.9 s). Dr. Ruth M. Deutsch's card glides up into place (Elementor fadeInUp)
   2.47  tap See Profile on her card
   2.60  her profile page is on screen ("Back to team", name, large arched photo, bio). Holds to 4.20.
   The cards below the fold were re-armed to their pre-reveal state with an IntersectionObserver
   (same trick as the desktop take), so the glide plays when they enter the view. The animation is
   the site's own CSS; only the trigger moment is ours.
   The network wait for the profile page is not captured (frames resume once it has loaded), so the
   cut from team to profile is instant. Its link is href="#" and navigates by script.
   The mobile menu was considered: it is a plain fade-in list with the hamburger turning into an X,
   which reads weaker than the profile transition.
