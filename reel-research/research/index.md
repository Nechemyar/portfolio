# Hero motion research (supahero.io)

Captured 28 Oct 2026 at 1440x900, dpr 1. For each site: `<slug>-entry.jpg` is the supahero entry page, `<slug>-1/2/3.jpg` are the live site 2.5 s apart (shot 1 is about 3 s after load, so some still show a preloader or cookie banner).

Ordered roughly from most to least useful as a reference for a showreel-style hero behind a headline.

| Slug | Live site | Motion and visual style |
